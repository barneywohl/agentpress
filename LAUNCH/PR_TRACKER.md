## Status legend

- `pending` — drafted but not filed
- `filed` — PR open, no review yet
- `reviewing` — maintainer engaged
- `changes_requested` — they want edits
- `approved` — approved, awaiting merge
- `merged` — landed (celebrate publicly)
- `closed` — declined (move on without arguing)
- `stale` — no response in 14+ days
